When a guest makes a booking, the system can check the room type, price and maximum guests to find a room that fits. Housekeeping can use the last_cleaned date to see which rooms need attention next. Later, a GUEST table and a BOOKING table could link to room_id, which would let the hotel track who is staying in which room and for how long, and calculate the total cost of a stay.
